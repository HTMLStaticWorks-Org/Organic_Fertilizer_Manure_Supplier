# Orgema — Organic Fertilizers & Manure

A responsive, six-page static website adapted from the supplied small-farm grocery website. The brand has been renamed Orgema and the content is focused on organic fertilizer and manure information.

## Pages

- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — About Orgema
- `products.html` — Vermicompost, Seaweed Extract, Neem Cake and Liquid Bio-NPK Consortium
- `crop-solutions.html` — Cereals & grains, horticulture & orchards, cash crops and home gardens
- `usage-guide.html` — Label-first application preparation, handling and record keeping
- `contact.html` — Bulk, product and distributor enquiries

## Run

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step is required. Google Fonts are loaded remotely when a connection is available; system fallbacks are included.

## Before publishing

1. Add verified company phone, email, address, service areas and legal business details.
2. Connect the contact form to a secure backend, email service or CRM. It currently demonstrates front-end validation only and does not send data.
3. Confirm actual product availability, formulations, nutrient analysis, batch details, pack sizes, pricing and label instructions.
4. Add only current, verifiable certificates and claims. No universal dosage rates, yield improvements or savings are asserted.
5. Replace any illustrative/source images with licensed, brand-approved photographs if required for commercial use.

## Design

Brand: Orgema. Palette: humus umber `#543A24`, sprout green `#65A30D`, light `#FBFBF7`, dark `#17150F`. Logo is the local SVG at `assets/images/logo.svg`. Responsive layout includes mobile navigation and a light/dark theme toggle.
