Product range

Choose inputs with clear intent.

Explore four product categories. Exact composition, availability, pack sizes and directions must be confirmed for each offered product.

Solid organic manure

Vermicompost

Organic material processed with earthworms. When comparing options, review feedstock, maturity, moisture, texture, screening and the declared nutrient analysis.

  • Ask for source and processing details.
  • Check moisture and maturity information.
  • Confirm pack size and storage guidance.
Seaweed-derived formulation

Seaweed Extract

Available formulations may differ in concentration, extraction method and physical form. Use the product's own label to determine compatibility and application directions.

  • Confirm liquid or powder form.
  • Review declared concentration and ingredients.
  • Check crop and application instructions.
Neem-based organic input

Neem Cake

Neem cake is a processed material remaining after neem seed oil extraction. Specifications can vary with source and processing.

  • Ask for source and processing details.
  • Review declared nutrient and quality parameters.
  • Follow label guidance for handling and application.
Microbial consortium

Liquid Bio-NPK Consortium

A microbial inoculant category intended to support nutrient-related biological processes. Organism identity, viable count, shelf life and crop directions must be product-specific.

  • Verify organism names and viable count.
  • Check expiry, storage and handling requirements.
  • Confirm compatibility and label directions.
