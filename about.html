About Orgema

Grounded in the value of healthy soil.

We bring organic fertilizer and manure categories together with clear information to support more informed growing decisions.

Our role

Make product choices easier to understand.

Organic inputs are not interchangeable. Their source materials, processing, nutrient profile, moisture, packaging and intended use can differ. Orgema is structured around helping growers understand those differences before placing an order.

We focus on product clarity, crop context and responsible communication rather than one-size-fits-all promises.

Two principles that guide us

Useful information. Responsible claims.

01

Specific over generic

We distinguish between product types and encourage buyers to review the actual label, technical sheet and batch information.

02

Evidence over promises

We do not assume yield gains, savings, certifications or nutrient values. Such details should only be published when supported by current records.

What matters to us

Respect for the realities of each farm.

Soil texture, rainfall, irrigation, crop variety, local practice and input history differ from field to field. Useful product guidance begins by recognising those differences.

We encourage growers to combine label directions with local agronomic advice and soil-test information where available.
