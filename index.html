Start with the field, not the label.

Crop stage, soil condition, irrigation, existing nutrient inputs and the product's own analysis all influence selection. Orgema's information is designed to help growers ask better questions before they apply an input.

  • Identify the crop and its current stage.
  • Review soil test results where available.
  • Compare the product label and batch details.

Seasonal rhythm

Organic inputs matched to the cropping calendar.

Field preparation, active vegetative growth and orchard cycles each call for different forms of organic matter and nutrient support.

Kharif & Monsoon

Basal soil conditioning

Incorporate well-cured vermicompost and neem cake during land preparation to build soil structure and moisture balance before sowing.

  • Supports early root establishment
  • Improves aeration in heavy soils
Rabi & Winter

Steady nutrient availability

Pair mature organic manures with microbial bio-NPK consortia when cooler soil temperatures slow natural nutrient release.

  • Supports rhizosphere activity
  • Fits irrigated grain and vegetable beds
Orchards & Perennials

Root-zone & canopy care

Combine ring-basin manure applications with periodic seaweed extract drenches or foliar sprays aligned to flowering and fruit set.

  • Suited to drip and basin irrigation
  • Aligned with post-harvest recovery
