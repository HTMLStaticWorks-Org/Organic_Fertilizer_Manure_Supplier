A practical approach

Start with the field, not the label.

Crop stage, soil condition, irrigation, existing nutrient inputs and the product's own analysis all influence selection. Orgema's information is designed to help growers ask better questions before they apply an input.

  • Identify the crop and its current stage.
  • Review soil test results where available.
  • Compare the product label and batch details.
