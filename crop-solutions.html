Crop solutions

Different crops. Different field conditions.

Use these growing segments as a starting point for product discussions — not as a substitute for crop-specific label directions.

Field crops

Cereals & grains

For paddy, wheat, maize and other grain systems, consider soil texture, crop stage, water management and existing nutrient plans.

Useful enquiry details: crop, acreage, planting stage and irrigation method.

Perennial crops

Horticulture & orchards

For fruit and plantation crops, account for tree age, canopy size, root-zone conditions, season and the product's stated placement method.

Useful enquiry details: crop variety, tree age, spacing and orchard area.

Commercial crops

Cash crops

For cotton, sugarcane, pulses and other commercial crops, align input selection with the production calendar and the broader nutrient programme.

Useful enquiry details: crop cycle, area, soil report and planned application window.

Small-scale growing

Home gardens

For containers, kitchen gardens and ornamental plants, pay attention to growing medium, drainage, plant sensitivity and pack convenience.

Useful enquiry details: plant type, container or bed, and growing medium.

Field planning

Five details that change the recommendation.

01

Soil texture

Coarse, medium and fine soils can behave differently with water and organic matter.

02

Crop stage

Land preparation, establishment and later growth stages have different needs.

03

Water regime

Rainfall, irrigation frequency and drainage affect field management.

04

Input history

Record previous manure, fertilizer and soil amendment applications.

05

Soil test

Use available test results to understand the starting conditions.

A note on recommendations

Crop category is only the beginning.

Two farms growing the same crop may need different input decisions. Product-specific labels, soil-test results and advice from a qualified local agronomist should guide actual rates and timing.

Orgema does not publish universal dosage rates or guaranteed yield outcomes.
